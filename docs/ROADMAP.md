# Roadmap

## Phase 1: Soul Prototype
Objective: Validate the emotional core of Little Dharma as a daily 10-minute ritual.
- Parent-first onboarding and trust framing.
- Child delight loop with story + shloka + reflection.
- Initial content universe schema and signature character tone.

## Phase 2: Private Beta
Objective: Validate repeat usage and trust with a curated parent cohort.
- Stable daily ritual journey and progress model.
- Festival and value-based content depth expansion.
- Parent safety controls and private sharing primitives.

## Phase 3: Paid Beta
Objective: Prove subscription willingness through premium family outcomes.
- Free-to-paid value ladder and trial experience.
- Enhanced audio rituals and multilingual growth.
- Retention instrumentation and early cohort economics.

## Phase 4: Public Launch
Objective: Launch with category-distinct quality and trust.
- Scaled content operations with review governance.
- Festival-led distribution engine and referral moments.
- Reliability, accessibility, and premium UX polish at scale.

## Phase 5: Category Leadership
Objective: Become a global benchmark in children’s culture-learning products.
- World-class IP expansion across worlds and characters.
- Deep parent community and educator/scholar ecosystem.
- Durable monetisation with high trust, high retention, and strong NPS.
