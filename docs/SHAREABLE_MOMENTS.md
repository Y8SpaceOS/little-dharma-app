# Shareable Moments Architecture

## Why Virality Must Be Parent-to-Parent (Not Child-to-Public)
Little Dharma growth should come from trusted parental networks, not child exposure. Parent-to-parent sharing preserves safety, improves conversion quality, and reinforces product trust.

## Allowed Share Moments
- Parent-approved milestone cards.
- Festival ritual completion cards.
- Family reflection prompts completed together.
- Child-created but parent-reviewed creative outputs.
- Invite-only family/community challenge participation.

## Blocked Unsafe Share Moments
- Any public child profile broadcasting.
- Auto-posting without explicit parent confirmation.
- Geolocation-linked child activity shares.
- Open comments or public social feed exposure.
- Real-time child status/activity visibility to unknown users.

## Festival-Led Sharing Architecture
- Seasonal campaign calendars with prebuilt share kits.
- Region/language-specific messaging variants.
- Ritual progression checkpoints tied to share moments.
- Parent-approved one-tap share exports to private channels.
- Post-festival recap cards to encourage retention, not one-time spikes.
