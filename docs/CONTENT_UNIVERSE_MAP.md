# Content Universe Map v1 (Sprint 35)

## Purpose
This document defines Little Dharma's content architecture for scaling from the current Vrindavan starter path toward a culturally respectful, parent-trusted, age-aware 1,000+ story universe.

This is a strategy/governance artifact, not a runtime feature spec. It does not authorize immediate app shell expansion, runtime story seeding, UI changes, or data model rollout.

## Scope and Constraints
- Docs-only sprint artifact.
- No runtime stories added.
- No app behavior/UI/navigation changes.
- No backend/auth/cloud sync/audio/analytics/native sharing changes.
- No unsupported scripture claims.

## Canonical Content Types
1. **Canonical story** — direct sacred/canonical narratives requiring highest care.
2. **Traditional retelling** — culturally grounded retellings from established tradition.
3. **Original Little Dharma story** — newly written values-first stories within respectful framing.
4. **Festival story** — festival origin/context/ritual-purpose stories.
5. **Bedtime reflection** — calm closure stories/prompts for nightly ritual.
6. **Parent-child ritual** — short guided relational practice moments.
7. **School/friendship dilemma** — modern child dilemmas with dharmic/value framing.
8. **Nature/gratitude story** — ecology, food, gratitude, and interdependence stories.
9. **Values practice story** — repeatable real-life value application stories.

## Source-Sensitivity Tiers
- **Tier 1 (High sensitivity canonical/sacred):** Canonical divine/sacred narratives; strongest review standards, conservative language, explicit source labeling.
- **Tier 2 (Traditional retellings):** Established retellings with regional variation; must preserve known core meaning and avoid fabricated claims.
- **Tier 3 (Cultural/festival context):** Practices, customs, significance framing; ensure regional humility and avoid universalizing one local practice.
- **Tier 4 (Original Little Dharma values stories):** Brand-authored value stories inspired by dharmic ethics without false canonical attribution.
- **Tier 5 (Everyday modern family/school):** Contemporary daily-life values moments; low scriptural risk but high pedagogy/tone quality requirement.

## Age-Band Strategy
### 3–5 years
- Parent-read, picture-led, very short.
- One feeling + one value per story.
- Minimal dilemma complexity; high warmth and repetition.

### 6–8 years (Core app path)
- Story + ritual + pause + quiz + carrying word.
- Clear value naming and practical application.
- Balanced delight + comprehension + calm pacing.

### 9–12 years
- Deeper dilemma framing and reasoning.
- Reflection prompts with parent discussion bridge.
- Nuanced choices, consequence thinking, and perspective-taking.

### Value Taxonomy Usage Rules
- Every story experience maps to **1 primary value** and optionally **1 supporting value**.
- Do not overload young-age stories with multiple equal moral lessons.
- Canonical/traditional stories should state values as interpretation support, not as reductive replacements for the story's sacred context.

## Recommended Sequencing (Roadmap Logic)
1. **Private beta:** Stay primarily Vrindavan-focused until ritual-loop quality, trust, and content consistency are proven.
2. **Then expand to Ganesha and Hanuman:** Only after Vrindavan quality bar is stably strong and editorial throughput is disciplined.
3. **Later-phased expansion:** Ramayana, Mahabharata, Shiva-Parvati, Devi, Saraswati, Panchatantra, and broad festival catalog should scale only after authenticity readiness milestones are met.
4. **Parallel support lane:** Everyday original stories + nature/family/school + bedtime/morning rituals can grow in controlled fashion to support habit and variety.

## Content Scale Targets (v1)
- **Base story idea pool:** 300–350.
- **Age-band variants per base story:** 3 (3–5, 6–8, 9–12).
- **Total potential experiences:** 900–1,050+.
- **Private beta target:** 21–30 stories (with strong depth, not breadth dumping).
- **Public beta target:** 60–100 story experiences.
- **Paid v1 target:** 150–250 story experiences.
- **Long-term moat target:** 1,000+ story experiences.

## Parent-Trust and Authenticity Safeguards
- Every story should carry visible **content label** (canonical / traditional retelling / original / festival / everyday).
- Canonical and traditional content should include conservative **source-note discipline** in authoring workflow.
- When traditions vary, state variation respectfully instead of asserting false certainty.
- Reflection prompts must guide without guilt, fear, or child-pressure framing.

## "Do Not Do Yet" Guardrails
- Do not scale by dumping low-quality AI-generated stories.
- Do not make unsupported scripture claims.
- Do not use sacred figures casually.
- Do not mix canonical, traditional, and original stories without labels.
- Do not add audio until pronunciation/authenticity QA exists.
- Do not add public child profiles, leaderboards, streak pressure, or chat/community.
- Do not collect child or parent names.

## Implementation Boundary for Sprint 35
This map is an architecture foundation only. Follow-on sprints may define authenticity OS, metadata model, content packets, and release sequencing, but Sprint 35 itself does not modify runtime content or product behavior.
