# Quality Levels and Release Gates

This ladder defines what “good enough” means by sprint phase. A sprint is complete only when it meets the target level’s bars and release criteria.

## Q1 — Functional Prototype
- **Sprint range:** Sprints 1–11
- **Product experience bar:** Core journey works end-to-end with basic clarity.
- **Content quality bar:** Safe draft content with basic coherence.
- **Ritual quality bar:** Ritual concept exists, not yet emotionally polished.
- **Parent trust bar:** No obvious trust violations; guardrails documented.
- **Child delight bar:** At least one moment of delight per core flow.
- **Safety/privacy bar:** No unsafe/public child surfaces.
- **Growth/shareability bar:** Value proposition understandable; sharing optional.
- **Monetisation readiness bar:** Hypothesis only.
- **Manual QA requirement:** Critical path smoke test on iPhone.
- **Parent testing requirement:** Optional exploratory feedback.
- **Content review requirement:** Internal review before merge.
- **Release gate criteria:** Demoable core loop and no critical safety blockers.

## Q2 — Soul Prototype
- **Sprint range:** Sprints 12–30
- **Product experience bar:** Calm, coherent, emotionally resonant daily loop.
- **Content quality bar:** Structured story packets with value clarity.
- **Ritual quality bar:** Story + value + shloka/ritual loop lands in 10 minutes.
- **Parent trust bar:** Parent language is explicit, conservative, and transparent.
- **Child delight bar:** Consistent delight cues across home, story, completion.
- **Safety/privacy bar:** Strong private-by-default behavior.
- **Growth/shareability bar:** Parent-safe private sharing narrative defined.
- **Monetisation readiness bar:** Retention signals and premium value narrative start.
- **Manual QA requirement:** Full iPhone critical path + regression around touched surfaces.
- **Parent testing requirement:** At least 3 qualitative parent sessions across phase.
- **Content review requirement:** Content quality bar + provenance checks.
- **Release gate criteria:** Gate A readiness evidence package complete.

## Q3 — Private Beta Quality
- **Sprint range:** Sprints 31–60
- **Product experience bar:** Reliable, low-friction daily usage for invited families.
- **Content quality bar:** Broader catalog with consistent authenticity and pedagogy.
- **Ritual quality bar:** Repeatable ritual cadence with clear parent bridge.
- **Parent trust bar:** Clear safeguards and predictable behavior under edge cases.
- **Child delight bar:** Delight persists beyond novelty week.
- **Safety/privacy bar:** Safety audits and incident-response playbook in place.
- **Growth/shareability bar:** Parent-to-parent sharing flow is safe and intentional.
- **Monetisation readiness bar:** Early paid hypotheses mapped; no trust compromise.
- **Manual QA requirement:** iPhone matrix across key devices/OS versions.
- **Parent testing requirement:** Recurring beta cohort reviews with logged feedback.
- **Content review requirement:** Editorial checklist pass per content drop.
- **Release gate criteria:** Gate B criteria met with documented blockers resolved.

## Q4 — Paid Beta Quality
- **Sprint range:** Sprints 61–90
- **Product experience bar:** Premium-feeling and dependable for paying families.
- **Content quality bar:** Higher depth, sequencing, and consistency.
- **Ritual quality bar:** Habit-forming 10-minute ritual reliability.
- **Parent trust bar:** Billing/value communication is transparent and fair.
- **Child delight bar:** Strong replayability and progression motivation.
- **Safety/privacy bar:** Privacy posture tested against growth surfaces.
- **Growth/shareability bar:** Share loops convert without safety regressions.
- **Monetisation readiness bar:** Paid conversion and retention instrumentation live.
- **Manual QA requirement:** Release-candidate manual iPhone QA per sprint.
- **Parent testing requirement:** Paid-beta cohort validation before phase gate.
- **Content review requirement:** Content + ritual authenticity sign-off.
- **Release gate criteria:** Gate C pass; hard blockers zero.

## Q5 — Public Launch Quality
- **Sprint range:** Sprints 91–120
- **Product experience bar:** Launch-grade polish and resilience.
- **Content quality bar:** Launch set is deep, authentic, and age-appropriate.
- **Ritual quality bar:** Ritual loop is clear, easy, and emotionally sticky.
- **Parent trust bar:** Trust promises are visible and verifiable.
- **Child delight bar:** Category-leading moment quality in core journeys.
- **Safety/privacy bar:** Public launch safety/privacy readiness demonstrated.
- **Growth/shareability bar:** Shareable moments are safe and compelling.
- **Monetisation readiness bar:** Pricing/value and renewal trust are launch-ready.
- **Manual QA requirement:** Full launch regression matrix on iPhone.
- **Parent testing requirement:** Representative pre-launch parent validation.
- **Content review requirement:** Launch catalog editorial and authenticity approval.
- **Release gate criteria:** Gate D launch readiness pass.

## Q6 — Category-Defining V1 Quality
- **Sprint range:** Sprints 121–150
- **Product experience bar:** Distinctive, benchmark-setting craft and cohesion.
- **Content quality bar:** Deep cultural breadth and editorial excellence.
- **Ritual quality bar:** Daily ritual becomes identity-level family habit.
- **Parent trust bar:** Industry-leading trust posture.
- **Child delight bar:** Signature magical moments with repeat ritual pull.
- **Safety/privacy bar:** Proactive safety leadership, not only compliance.
- **Growth/shareability bar:** Trusted, parent-led advocacy flywheel.
- **Monetisation readiness bar:** Durable value and retention economics.
- **Manual QA requirement:** Category-defining quality review on flagship iPhones.
- **Parent testing requirement:** Ongoing longitudinal parent cohorts.
- **Content review requirement:** Editorial board-style quality governance.
- **Release gate criteria:** Gate E category-defining review pass.

## Phase Gates

### Gate A — After Sprint 30: Soul Prototype Review
- **Required product proof:** End-to-end 10-minute ritual loop feels coherent.
- **Required content proof:** Structured packets with quality bar compliance.
- **Required trust/safety proof:** No risky child-public surfaces; trust copy validated.
- **Required manual iPhone QA:** Core journeys + regressions logged.
- **Required parent testing:** Early parent interviews with actionable themes.
- **Required analytics/retention proof:** Basic completion/returning usage signals.
- **Hard blockers:** Critical safety/privacy issue, broken critical path, major content inaccuracies.
- **Advisory blockers:** Weak polish, unclear share framing, limited ritual clarity.

### Gate B — After Sprint 60: Private Beta Readiness
- **Required product proof:** Stable private-beta experience with low-friction use.
- **Required content proof:** Multi-journey depth and consistency.
- **Required trust/safety proof:** Safety checklist and incident process documented.
- **Required manual iPhone QA:** Device matrix and issue triage evidence.
- **Required parent testing:** Beta cohort feedback cycles completed.
- **Required analytics/retention proof:** Early retention and habit indicators.
- **Hard blockers:** Safety regressions, severe reliability defects, unreviewed content.
- **Advisory blockers:** Uneven delight quality, weak progression cues.

### Gate C — After Sprint 90: Paid Beta Readiness
- **Required product proof:** Paid-grade polish in core loops.
- **Required content proof:** Strong ritual/content variety and authenticity checks.
- **Required trust/safety proof:** Growth + monetisation flows preserve trust.
- **Required manual iPhone QA:** Release-candidate QA with pass/fail records.
- **Required parent testing:** Paid-beta willingness and value validation.
- **Required analytics/retention proof:** Conversion + retention funnel visibility.
- **Hard blockers:** Billing trust risk, critical defects, safety/privacy failures.
- **Advisory blockers:** Low differentiation or inconsistent premium taste.

### Gate D — After Sprint 120: Public Launch Readiness
- **Required product proof:** Launch-level reliability and craft.
- **Required content proof:** Launch catalog depth and review sign-off.
- **Required trust/safety proof:** Public-facing trust claims backed by behavior.
- **Required manual iPhone QA:** Full launch checklist completed.
- **Required parent testing:** Representative launch-readiness validation.
- **Required analytics/retention proof:** Stable activation and repeat-use baselines.
- **Hard blockers:** Launch-blocking defects or trust/safety gaps.
- **Advisory blockers:** Non-critical polish inconsistencies.

### Gate E — After Sprint 150: Category-Defining V1 Review
- **Required product proof:** Category benchmark quality across core surfaces.
- **Required content proof:** Distinctive cultural depth and editorial quality.
- **Required trust/safety proof:** Trust moat demonstrated in practice.
- **Required manual iPhone QA:** Flagship quality audit with evidence.
- **Required parent testing:** Longitudinal habit and advocacy signals.
- **Required analytics/retention proof:** Strong retention and parent-led growth indicators.
- **Hard blockers:** Any severe trust/safety/privacy risk; shallow core loop quality.
- **Advisory blockers:** Gaps that reduce category-defining differentiation.
