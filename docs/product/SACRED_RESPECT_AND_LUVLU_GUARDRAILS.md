# Sacred Respect and Luvlu Guardrails

## Purpose

Little Dharma handles sacred stories for children. This requires warmth, restraint, and cultural respect. This document defines how sacred figures, sacred stories, and Luvlu should be used.

## Sacred content principles

- Treat sacred figures with reverence and warmth.
- Avoid mockery, sarcasm, or comedy framing of sacred figures.
- Avoid fear-heavy, violence-heavy, revenge-heavy, or punishment-heavy storytelling.
- Use child-safe versions for intense stories.
- Do not over-cartoon sacred figures.
- Do not turn sacred figures into game characters, tokens, or collectible rewards.
- Do not use sacred imagery as a reward mechanic.
- Do not place mascot chatter over sacred sound, shloka moments, or devotional focal art.

## Editorial treatment by theme

### Krishna

Krishna Childhood stories may be playful, loving, devotional, and warm. Playfulness must not become prank-heavy comedy. Butter stories should show innocence, family love, sharing, truth, or repair.

### Ramayana

Ramayana stories should emphasize dharma, courage, family love, duty, humility, service, and compassion. Intense war or exile moments should be child-safe and not graphic.

### Ganesha

Ganesha stories should emphasize wisdom, listening, beginnings, humility, patience, and obstacle-solving. Ganesha should not be treated as a comic device.

### Hanuman

Hanuman stories should emphasize devotion, courage, service, humility, loyalty, and calm strength. Strength should be framed as service, not domination.

### Bhagavad Gita for Children

The Bhagavad Gita requires extra editorial care. It should be framed around child-friendly ideas such as courage, duty, calm, kindness, focus, and doing the right thing. Avoid adult theological complexity unless carefully adapted.

### Mahabharata child-safe

Mahabharata stories require child-safe selection and framing. Avoid graphic conflict, revenge, humiliation, and morally confusing detail without parent context.

## Luvlu role

Luvlu is the peacock helper, guide, and narrator layer. Luvlu should make the app feel warmer and easier to navigate.

Approved uses:

- onboarding guidance
- empty states
- gentle hints
- story transitions
- calm practice prompts
- parent-child discussion prompts
- completion encouragement
- bedtime quiet guidance

Prohibited uses:

- Luvlu as logo
- Luvlu as deity
- Luvlu as sacred authority
- Luvlu as reward token
- Luvlu interrupting sacred art or shloka
- Luvlu creating jokes around sacred figures
- Luvlu driving XP, streaks, coins, rankings, or hard gamification

## Luvlu tone

Luvlu should sound warm, simple, respectful, and child-friendly. Luvlu should not over-explain, over-celebrate, or dominate the screen.

## Placement rules

Luvlu can appear in supportive areas of the screen but should not cover deity art, sacred focal content, story text, parent trust copy, or important CTAs.

## Future IP boundary

Luvlu can become a branded companion over time, but must remain separate from sacred figures. The product brand mark remains diya-lotus-sun. Luvlu is a helper within the experience, not the brand mark.
