# Post-Foundation Product Build Roadmap

## Context and Decision
Sprint 1–60 established and validated the foundation. **Foundation Phase complete** means repo artifacts, governance docs, and validators are in place; it does not mean launch readiness. Sprint 60 was a release-candidate documentation gate and remains in a **conditional no-go** state pending complete **manual iPhone QA** evidence.

The owner decision for Sprint 61 is to pause immediate paid-beta and external-private-beta expansion. We are **not paid beta** now, we are **not external private beta** now, and we will **continue building the app** deeply before distribution moves.

## Why Sprint 60 Is Foundation-Complete, Not Launch-Complete
- Sprint 60 closed documentation and gate artifact readiness, not market readiness.
- Manual iPhone QA evidence remains incomplete for a go decision.
- Product depth, parent utility, and repeat-use loops still need substantial iteration.
- Launch-critical trust, quality, and content sufficiency standards are not yet fully proven at scale.

## Why Paid Beta / External Private Beta Are Paused
- Governance-first sequencing reduces trust and quality risk.
- Current decision is to invest in core product depth before widening distribution.
- Premature release could produce low retention, weak habit formation, and avoidable parent trust erosion.

## Product-Build Philosophy (Post-Foundation)
- Build depth before reach.
- Protect trust and safety constraints while increasing child delight and parent value.
- Prove repeat ritual quality and operational QA discipline before expansion.
- Treat each phase gate as a decision checkpoint, not an automatic release trigger.

## Phase 2 Goals — Product Build Phase 2 (Sprints 62–90)
- Improve child onboarding/profile setup and journey clarity.
- Improve story browsing/discovery and detail depth.
- Improve ritual/review/completion loop emotional quality.
- Deepen My Treasures and Values Garden usefulness.
- Raise visual polish and emotional richness.
- Prepare manual iPhone QA evidence packs without external release.

## Phase 3 Goals — Content + Parent Utility Expansion (Sprints 91–120)
- Expand high-quality content breadth with QA discipline.
- Improve parent utility and repeat usage value.
- Strengthen family ritual loops and trust surfaces.
- Advance content operations maturity with explicit safeguards.

## Phase 4 Goals — Distribution-Ready Product Build (Sprints 121–150)
- Finalize app quality and manual QA evidence.
- Complete screenshots, metadata, policy readiness, and onboarding polish.
- Confirm content sufficiency and product-led habit quality.
- Keep external beta as a separate explicit owner decision.

## Preconditions Before External Private Beta
- Manual iPhone QA matrix complete with reproducible evidence.
- Accessibility and critical-flow regression packs current and passing.
- Parent trust surfaces and privacy claims verified against runtime behavior.
- Sufficient content depth and consistent daily ritual loop quality validated.
- Owner explicit go decision recorded.

## Preconditions Before Paid Beta
- External private beta learning loop completed and stable.
- Retention, trust, and support burden thresholds reviewed by owner.
- Monetisation policy, value narrative, and ethical guardrails explicitly approved.
- Release readiness artifacts, legal/policy checks, and QA evidence fully green.
- Owner explicit paid-beta go decision recorded.

## Top Product Gaps Remaining
- Onboarding/profile depth and age-fit setup quality.
- Discovery and journey-map clarity at growing content scale.
- Story-detail emotional richness and ritual consistency.
- Parent configuration/control surfaces and weekly utility depth.
- Content-pack architecture robustness for future expansion.
- Cross-device manual QA evidence completeness and performance polish.

## Risks of Launching Too Early
- Parent trust damage from quality inconsistencies.
- Weak child habit formation due to shallow loop quality.
- Support burden spikes from insufficient readiness.
- Content quality drift under expansion pressure.
- Brand/reputation risk from avoidable reliability issues.
