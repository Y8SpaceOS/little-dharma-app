# Content Quality Bar

Little Dharma content must meet this bar before shipping.

## 1) Age appropriateness
- Language, pacing, and emotional tone must match target age band.
- No fear-heavy, shame-heavy, or developmentally confusing framing.

## 2) Cultural accuracy
- Festivals, stories, names, and practices must be culturally grounded and respectful.
- Avoid flattening regional diversity into one monolithic narrative.

## 3) Attribution integrity
- Scripture, shloka, and quote provenance must be verified before explicit attribution.
- If provenance is uncertain, label as adaptation/chant seed and avoid false claims.

## 4) Parent trust
- Parent should feel content is safe, respectful, and values-aligned.
- No manipulative framing, no hidden upsell pressure inside child flow.

## 5) Child delight
- Content should feel warm, memorable, and repeatable.
- Include moments of wonder, agency, and simple ritual participation.

## 6) No false scriptural claims
- Never present invented lines as canonical scripture.
- Clearly separate adaptation language from canonical references.

## 7) No content-farm tone
- Avoid generic, SEO-like, or low-effort templated voice.
- Maintain handcrafted, premium, trustworthy storytelling quality.

## 8) Review workflow
1. Draft content against age/value objective.
2. Self-review against this quality bar.
3. Cultural/scriptural attribution review.
4. Parent trust/safety review.
5. In-app read-through for rhythm and delight.
6. Final sign-off with decision log note for any exceptions.
