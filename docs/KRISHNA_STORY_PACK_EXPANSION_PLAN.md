# Krishna Story Pack Expansion Plan (Sprint 73)

## Product objective
Build a safe, structured expansion plan for the **Krishna Story Pack** before adding new runtime stories.

## Child-facing experience objective
Create a warm Krishna Stories world and story path language for children: Krishna Childhood, Vrindavan, butter, flute, friendship, kindness, courage, love, care, truth, devotion, blessings, diyas, flowers.

## Parent-trust objective
Krishna stories are planned as **child-safe retellings** and **parent-trusted** value stories. Some episodes need older-child framing.

## Krishna Story Pack and Krishna Childhood Journey relation
- **Krishna Stories / Story World:** browse-friendly standalone story experiences.
- **Krishna Childhood Journey:** sequential path from birth and early childhood through key Vrindavan/Braj moments.
- Journey implementation is future scope; this sprint only plans sequence and metadata.

## Recommended story sequencing (planning)
1. Birth and early care episodes.
2. Gokul/Vrindavan playful childhood episodes.
3. Friendship, courage, and protection episodes.
4. Transition episodes (including Mathura departure) for older children.

## Age bands planning principles
- **3–5:** simple wonder, care, family warmth, kindness; no fear-heavy detail.
- **6–8:** friendship, courage, responsibility, gentle conflict resolution.
- **9–12:** deeper meaning, devotion, duty, and moral nuance with child-safe language.

## Source-tradition and editorial guardrails
- Use source-tradition respect and note retelling context.
- Depict Krishna with warmth, reverence, and child-safe wonder.
- No deity trivialisation.
- No sectarian superiority.
- No caste-coded messaging.
- No guilt-based devotion.
- No fear-based religion.
- No graphic violence.
- Butter/flute/play episodes must carry gentle values.
- Difficult episodes require soft framing and parent notes.
- Keep language simple and emotionally warm.

## Story metadata requirements
Each planned story should include:
- storyId, title, childFacingTitle, proposedOrder
- contentSurface (story_world, krishna_childhood_journey, or both)
- ageBands
- primaryValue, secondaryValues
- characters
- sourceTradition
- sensitivityLevel
- editorialNotes
- status

## Parent-facing trust summary
- Krishna stories are planned as child-safe retellings.
- Some episodes need older-child framing.
- The app will avoid fear-based religion, graphic violence, and sectarian superiority.
- Stories will connect to kindness, courage, love, truth, gratitude, and devotion.

## Content QA checklist
- Plan doc exists and references Krishna Story Pack + Krishna Childhood Journey.
- CSV plan has required columns and at least 20 planned stories.
- Age bands and editorial guardrails are explicitly documented.
- Planned rows are not marked as published runtime content.
- Existing Vrindavan content and Content Pack Architecture remain preserved.

## Future phases
1. Metadata and editorial review pass.
2. Story drafting batches by age band.
3. Parent review and sensitivity pass.
4. Controlled runtime integration with existing Story World/Journey models.

## Explicit non-scope for Sprint 73
- No full new Krishna story pack runtime content.
- No full journey runtime build.
- No audio playback, microphone, or recording.
- No backend/auth/cloud sync/CMS.
- No analytics/telemetry.
- No paid beta, external private beta, monetisation, or App Store submission work.
