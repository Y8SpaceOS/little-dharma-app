# Ganesha World Architecture v1 (Sprint 74)

## Product objective
Define the architecture foundation for a warm, child-facing **Ganesha World** in Little Dharma without adding full runtime Ganesha story content.

## Child-facing Story World language
The Ganesha area should feel like a gentle Story World with language such as:
- story world
- gentle story paths
- wisdom
- blessings
- diyas and flowers
- focus, care, courage, and gratitude
- little treasures of learning

Keep Luvlu light-touch: short, optional helper nudges only.

## Ganesha World structure (planning)
1. **Start Here card:** one short parent-trusted intro to Ganesha wisdom for children.
2. **Gentle Story Paths:** pathways grouped by beginnings, listening, focus, kindness, and responsibility.
3. **Age-aware lanes:** same themes adapted for 3–5, 6–8, 9–12.
4. **Parent Notes layer:** concise trust note for episodes requiring contextual framing.
5. **Treasure bridge:** optional soft bridge to My Treasures reflection language (no gamification mechanics).

## Relation to Ganesha Wisdom Journey
- **Ganesha Stories / Story World:** browse-friendly, non-linear story corners.
- **Ganesha Wisdom Journey:** structured sequence for future Dharma Journey progression.
- This sprint defines taxonomy and sequencing intent only; no runtime journey build is included.

## Source-tradition and editorial guardrails
- Respect source-tradition context and avoid flattening sacred meaning.
- Keep deity framing reverent, warm, and child-safe.
- No deity trivialisation.
- No sectarian superiority.
- No caste-coded messaging.
- No guilt-based devotion.
- No fear-based religion.
- No graphic violence.
- Symbolic episodes should be framed as values-centered learning, not fear pressure.
- Complex episodes must include soft framing notes for parents.

## Age-band suitability principles
- **3–5:** simple warmth, listening, kindness, gratitude, and beginnings language only.
- **6–8:** persistence, problem-solving, friendship, and responsibility with gentle conflict handling.
- **9–12:** deeper symbolism, discernment, humility, and value reflection with clear emotional safety.

## Parent-trust notes
- Planned as child-safe retellings with calm, values-first language.
- Local-first architecture remains intact (no cloud dependency).
- No public profile, no child social exposure, no ranking/competition surfaces.
- Sensitive stories are flagged for older-child framing.

## Metadata and planning artifact requirements
Each planned row should include:
- storyId, title, childFacingTitle, proposedOrder
- contentSurface (story_world, ganesha_wisdom_journey, or both)
- ageBands
- primaryValue, secondaryValues
- characters
- sourceTradition
- sensitivityLevel
- editorialNotes
- status
