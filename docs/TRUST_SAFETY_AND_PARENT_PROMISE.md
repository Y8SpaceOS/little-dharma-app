# Trust, Safety, and Parent Promise

## Parent-Owned Account Principle
The parent is the primary account owner and decision-maker for profile setup, permissions, sharing, and data controls.

## No Ads
Little Dharma will not include third-party advertising units or ad-targeting systems.

## No Open Chat
No open child-to-public or child-to-stranger messaging surfaces.

## No Public Child Profile
Child identity and progress are private by default and never publicly indexable.

## No Public Leaderboard
No global/public ranking systems for children.

## Parent-Controlled Sharing
All outbound sharing requires explicit parent action and review.

## Child Data Minimisation
Collect only minimum required data for functionality, with retention limits and deletion controls.

## Age-Appropriate Content Rules
- Age-banded language and complexity.
- Sensitive topics adapted with child-safe framing.
- Cultural context presented with care and plurality where relevant.

## Scholar/Educator Review Direction
Establish a structured review council or advisor workflow for high-impact cultural/religious content, ensuring authenticity, inclusivity, and developmental appropriateness.
