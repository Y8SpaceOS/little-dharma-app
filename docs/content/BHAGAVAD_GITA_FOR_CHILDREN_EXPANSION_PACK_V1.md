# Bhagavad Gita for Children Expansion Pack v1

## Purpose

This pack expands child-safe Bhagavad Gita coverage with warm, simple, practical stories for Little Dharma.

## Scope delivered

- 100 indexed story experiences
- 35 qa_ready runtime candidates
- 30 audio-script-ready candidates
- category: `bhagavad_gita_for_children`
- journey: `bhagavad-gita-for-children-journey-v1`
- no runtime_ready promotion

## Editorial bar

This pack is sacred/philosophical content for children and is intentionally:

- warm
- simple
- non-preachy
- non-violent in framing
- practical and child-facing

### Focus values

- courage
- calm
- duty and responsibility
- doing the right thing
- kindness
- focus
- humility
- devotion
- self-control
- gratitude
- friendship
- steady effort

### Avoided framing

- battlefield intensity
- fear-heavy language
- adult theological density
- sectarian assertions
- moral lecturing
- abstract philosophy without child-facing examples
- generic panel shells
- repeated panel/narration skeletons

## Validation expectations

Validator checks include:

- exactly 100 unique IDs
- at least 35 qa_ready stories
- at least 30 audio-script-ready stories
- no empty panels
- audioMetadata present on all stories
- journey registration in registry
- no duplicate IDs against existing content
- Story Experience Index validation passes
- no changed files outside approved sprint scope
- no route/runtime/story-reader/story-completion/journey-progress/audio/backend/analytics/sharing/notification/mic/TTS/AI/gamification drift
- no generic panel phrases
- no repeated panel openings
- no repeated narration openings
- no child-facing numbered titles
- no battlefield/fear-heavy/adult-theology language
