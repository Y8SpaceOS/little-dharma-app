# Mahabharata Child-Safe Expansion Pack v1

## Scope

- Adds 100 indexed Mahabharata child-safe story experiences.
- Adds 35 qa_ready runtime candidates.
- Adds 25 audio-script-ready candidates.
- Category: `mahabharata_child_safe`.
- Journey: `mahabharata-child-safe-journey-v1`.
- No runtime_ready promotion.

## Editorial bar

This pack is child-safe, emotionally warm, non-violent in framing, and values-first.

Focus values:
- friendship
- fairness
- patience
- courage
- truth
- humility
- wisdom
- family responsibility
- choices
- consequences
- dharma in simple child-facing language

Avoided framing:
- battle intensity
- revenge
- humiliation
- gore
- heavy politics
- adult moral complexity
- fear-heavy framing

## Notes

- Runtime candidates remain `qa_ready` and are not promoted to `runtime_ready`.
- Audio scripts are metadata-only readiness assets; no playback or TTS behavior changes are included.
