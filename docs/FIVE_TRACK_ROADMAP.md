# Five-Track Roadmap

This roadmap reframes execution into five parallel tracks so the product scales as a category leader, not a narrow feature app.

## Track 1: World-Class Product Experience
Goal: Build a premium, calm, intuitive daily ritual experience for parent and child.

Milestones:
- Parent-first onboarding clarity and trust signals.
- Child home optimized for 10-minute daily loops.
- High-craft interaction language, motion, typography, and audio cues.
- Offline-resilient essential journeys.
- Accessibility and multilingual UX baseline.

## Track 2: Deep Content Engine
Goal: Create a structured, expanding Hindu wisdom content universe with durable metadata.

Milestones:
- Content universe schema (worlds, values, festivals, shlokas, rituals, quizzes).
- Editorial workflow and source-note discipline.
- Age-band and language variants.
- Character-arc sequencing and seasonal content calendars.
- Content quality scoring and review governance.

## Track 3: Parent Trust and Child Safety
Goal: Make trust and safety the strongest product moat.

Milestones:
- Parent-owned account model.
- No-ads/no-open-chat/no-public-child-surface enforcement.
- Privacy-by-design and data minimization policies.
- Parent control center for sharing and usage visibility.
- Scholar/educator review mechanisms for sensitive content.

## Track 4: Viral Share Moments and Festival-Led Distribution
Goal: Drive organic growth through parent-to-parent pride sharing and festival rituals.

Milestones:
- Parent-safe share card system.
- Festival campaign architecture (Diwali, Navratri, Janmashtami, etc.).
- Ritual completion and achievement share templates.
- Referral loops rooted in value, not spam.
- Regional language share copy library.

## Track 5: Subscription Economics and Community Launch Strategy
Goal: Build durable monetization with high retention and trust.

Milestones:
- Free-to-paid value narrative anchored in family outcomes.
- Pricing experiments by market and plan cadence.
- Trial-to-subscription experience design.
- Parent ambassador/community pilots.
- Cohort retention instrumentation and churn insight loops.
