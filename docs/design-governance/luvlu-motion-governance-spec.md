# Luvlu Motion Governance Spec (Screens 424-487)

Screens 424-487 are governance references only. They are not runtime app routes.

## Canonical Look
Luvlu has one approved base look and may not drift into mascot variation without explicit approval.

## Approved Poses
Poses remain calm, kind, and non-dominant relative to sacred focal art.

## Approved Emotions
Emotions are supportive and never manipulative, reward-seeking, or urgency-driven.

## Size Rules
Luvlu remains secondary to the learning and story content.

## Placement Rules
Luvlu must not cover sacred focal art and must respect copy readability.

## Bubble Styles
Speech bubbles are concise, optional, and parent-trust aligned.

## Do Not Use Rules
- no XP/coins/streaks/leaderboards
- no microphone/recording
- no sacred focal art overlay

## Luvlu and Future Friends
No future companions are allowed without explicit approval.

## Companion Selection Future State
Any future companion selection flow requires product and parent-trust governance review.

## One Primary Guide Rule
Only one primary on-screen guide should be active at a time.

## Luvlu vs Narrator Voice
Luvlu guidance must not replace narrator or guardian voice.

## Merchandise / IP Reference
Luvlu usage is educational and in-product; merchandise/IP extensions require separate approval.

## Not Logo, Not Deity
Luvlu is not logo. Luvlu is not deity.

## Motion Accessibility
Reduced-motion fallback is required where motion is present.

## Parent Boundary Copy
Parent boundary copy guardrails are required for sensitive or limit-setting guidance.
