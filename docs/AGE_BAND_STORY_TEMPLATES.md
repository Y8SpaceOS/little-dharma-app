# Age-Band Story Templates v1

## Purpose
Age-Band Story Templates v1 defines a repeatable content architecture that turns one `BaseStoryIdea` into three distinct, age-appropriate `StoryExperience` variants (`ages_3_5`, `ages_6_8`, `ages_9_12`) without shallow copy/paste duplication.

This template system exists to:
- Make story production repeatable for writers, LLM drafting flows, and human reviewers.
- Ensure age appropriateness in tone, vocabulary, emotional load, ritual depth, and reflection style.
- Prevent “same story, minor wording changes” outputs across age bands.
- Preserve authenticity, source discipline, and parent trust at scale.
- Support the 300–350 base story ideas × 3 age bands strategy (900–1,050+ story experiences).

---

## Primary Age Bands
- `ages_3_5`
- `ages_6_8`
- `ages_9_12`

Each age band is treated as a separate story experience design problem, not a text simplification pass.

## Age-Band Template: `ages_3_5`

### Experience intent
Parent-read, picture-led, emotionally safe micro-story that supports bedtime and bonding.

### Format rules
- Parent-read format (adult voice with simple child echo lines).
- Picture-led / image-friendly structure (clear visual beats).
- Very short total length (typically 4–6 panels; 1–2 short lines per panel).
- Exactly one feeling + one value.
- Include 1–2 simple repeated phrases (comfort rhythm).
- No moral burden language (“you must,” “good kids always”).
- No guilt, fear, punishment, spiritual threat, or abstract philosophy.

### Parent participation style
- Parent co-regulation: read slowly, smile, mirror calm breathing.
- Invite child to repeat one short line.
- Keep correction-free; no performance pressure.

### Ritual style
- 1 micro-action + 1 line (e.g., hand-on-heart + short phrase).
- 20–45 seconds total.
- No precision requirements or memorization burden.

### Pause style
- 1 breath pause.
- Prompt style: sensory/emotional (“Did your heart feel warm?”).
- 5–15 seconds.

### Quiz/reflection style
- No complex quiz.
- At most one playful either/or or pointing prompt.

### Carrying word style
- Single concrete warm word (e.g., “Kind,” “Calm,” “Share”).
- Parent says: “Today we carry ___.”

### Bedtime fit
- Calm close, safety language, no unresolved tension.
- Child leaves with reassurance, not challenge burden.

### Example template (`ages_3_5`)
- **Story title:**
- **Age band:** ages_3_5
- **Content label:** story_retelling_light
- **Source sensitivity tier:** Tier 3 (adapted moral retelling)
- **Source note:** Inspired by devotional storytelling tradition; child-safe adaptation.
- **Parent source note:** This is a values-first adaptation, not a literal scripture translation.
- **Primary value:** Kindness
- **Carrying word:** Kind
- **Story arc:** A friend is sad → child notices → small kind action → warm resolution.
- **Panels / beats:** 4–6 short beats, one image idea each.
- **Ritual:** Hand on heart; say “Kind heart, kind hands.”
- **Pause:** “One soft breath. Did your heart feel gentle?”
- **Quiz or reflection:** “Should we use gentle hands or stompy hands?”
- **Badge / treasure:** Gentle Helper Leaf
- **Parent mirror prompt:** “Where did we see kindness today?”
- **Bedtime reflection:** “Tonight we sleep with a kind heart.”
- **Review notes:** Verify no fear, no shame, no abstract lecture.

## Age-Band Template: `ages_6_8`

### Experience intent
Core app loop format with simple arc and gentle reflection: Story + Ritual + Pause + Quiz + Carrying Word.

### Format rules
- This is the primary private-beta production format.
- Story + ritual + pause + quiz + carrying word are all required.
- Simple narrative arc: setup → challenge → choice → outcome → value naming.
- One primary value required; one light supporting value optional.

### Quiz/reflection style
- Gentle quiz (2–3 prompts).
- Prefer meaning-making over “gotcha correctness.”
- Child should feel guided, not judged.

### Parent mirror format
- 1 practical parent question tied to daily life.
- Encourage short family conversation (30–90 seconds).

### Bedtime reflection format
- 1 calming recap line + 1 “tomorrow practice” line.

### Ritual phrase rules
- 1–2 short lines.
- Understandable without doctrinal background.
- No unsupported sacred claims.

### Carrying word rules
- One clear, actionable value word.
- Child can use it at school/home the same day.

### Example template (`ages_6_8`)
- **Story title:**
- **Age band:** ages_6_8
- **Content label:** story_ritual_quiz_core
- **Source sensitivity tier:** Tier 2 or Tier 3 depending on adaptation depth
- **Source note:** Values-oriented retelling with authenticity review.
- **Parent source note:** Language adjusted for age comprehension; source lineage noted conservatively.
- **Primary value:** Courage
- **Carrying word:** Courage
- **Story arc:** A child hesitates to help → remembers value → takes one brave action → sees positive impact.
- **Panels / beats:** 6–8 beats, short sentences, concrete actions.
- **Ritual:** “Steady breath, steady heart. I can do one brave thing kindly.”
- **Pause:** 10–20 seconds: “What did the brave choice change?”
- **Quiz or reflection:** 2–3 gentle prompts, one best-fit answer each.
- **Badge / treasure:** Brave Heart Lantern
- **Parent mirror prompt:** “What is one small brave choice our family can practice tonight?”
- **Bedtime reflection:** “Today we practiced courage. Tomorrow we try it in one small moment again.”
- **Review notes:** Check tone warmth, no harsh scoring language, no fear framing.

## Age-Band Template: `ages_9_12`

### Experience intent
Less babyish, more reflective moral-reasoning format with internal conflict, perspective-taking, and parent discussion.

### Format rules
- Deeper dilemma structure (not just obvious right/wrong).
- Include internal conflict / choice / consequence without shame.
- Encourage perspective-taking (self, friend, family, community).
- Tone should respect growing autonomy.

### Reflection-first quiz style
- Reflection question first, then optional structured choices.
- Allow reasonable ambiguity when ethically appropriate.
- Reward thoughtful reasoning, not compliance.

### Parent discussion format
- Richer parent mirror prompt (values + context + consequences).
- Invite listening before advising.

### Carrying word / phrase style
- May be one word or short phrase (“Steady Truth,” “Courage with Kindness”).
- Should guide decisions, not label identity.

### Example template (`ages_9_12`)
- **Story title:**
- **Age band:** ages_9_12
- **Content label:** story_reflection_dialogue
- **Source sensitivity tier:** Tier 2/3 with explicit adaptation notes
- **Source note:** Moral-theme adaptation reviewed for fidelity and safety.
- **Parent source note:** Not a verbatim scriptural quotation; interpretation-oriented for preteens.
- **Primary value:** Integrity
- **Carrying word:** Steady Truth
- **Story arc:** Child faces social pressure → weighs belonging vs honesty → makes a costly truthful choice → reflects on consequence and dignity.
- **Panels / beats:** 7–10 beats with thought + dialogue balance.
- **Ritual:** Breath + phrase: “Steady mind, steady truth.”
- **Pause:** 20–30 seconds: “Which choice cost more, and why?”
- **Quiz or reflection:** Reflection-first + 2 follow-up reasoning prompts.
- **Badge / treasure:** Truth Compass
- **Parent mirror prompt:** “When is honesty hard in real life, and how can we support truth without punishment fear?”
- **Bedtime reflection:** “What truth will I carry tomorrow, even if it feels difficult?”
- **Review notes:** Ensure no preachy superiority tone; preserve dignity and complexity.

## BaseStoryIdea → Age-Variant Transformation Model

### Core principle
- `BaseStoryIdea` remains one shared concept.
- Each age band receives a distinct `StoryExperience` implementation.

### Transformation rules
- Value may remain constant, but framing must change by developmental stage.
- Ritual deepens with age (from imitation → intention → introspection).
- Parent prompt shifts from co-reading to guided dialogue.
- Quiz/reflection shifts from playful check → gentle understanding → moral reasoning.
- Carrying word evolves from concrete single word to nuanced phrase where appropriate.

### Anti-duplication requirement
If three variants read like paraphrases of each other, template quality fails. Each variant must feel native to its age band.

## Illustrative Transformation Example (Illustrative Only; Not Runtime Data)

**BaseStoryIdea:** “A child notices someone left out and chooses to include them.”

> Illustrative only for template guidance. Do not add this directly to runtime story seed data.

### Variant outline: `ages_3_5`
- Feeling: “Lonely”
- Value: “Kindness”
- Beats: See sad face → hold hand → play together.
- Ritual: “Kind heart, kind hands.”
- Pause: “One soft breath.”
- Quiz style: one simple either/or.
- Carrying word: “Kind.”
- Source labeling: Tier 4 original values story acceptable; no scripture claim.

### Variant outline: `ages_6_8`
- Value focus: Kindness (+ optional inclusion)
- Beats: Notice exclusion → choose action despite hesitation → group welcomes.
- Ritual: “I can make space with kindness.”
- Pause: “What changed when someone made space?”
- Quiz style: 2–3 gentle prompts.
- Carrying word: “Include.”
- Source labeling: If inspired by a traditional motif, use conservative adaptation note + parentSourceNote.

### Variant outline: `ages_9_12`
- Dilemma: Include someone and risk peer disapproval.
- Beats: Social pressure → internal debate → principled choice → mixed consequence + dignity.
- Ritual: “Steady kindness, even when it is not popular.”
- Pause: “What risk felt real, and what mattered more?”
- Reflection style: reflection-first reasoning prompts.
- Carrying phrase: “Courageous Inclusion.”
- Source labeling: No invented quotes, no unsupported scripture attribution; interpretation explicitly labeled.

## Age-Band Writing Rules Matrix

| Dimension | ages_3_5 | ages_6_8 | ages_9_12 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sentence length | Very short; 4–8 words typical | Short; 8–14 words typical | Mixed; concise but can handle layered thoughts |
| Vocabulary level | Concrete everyday words | Mostly concrete + light abstract values terms | Broader emotional/ethical vocabulary |
| Emotional complexity | One feeling at a time | One core feeling + simple mixed feeling | Mixed feelings, conflict, tradeoff awareness |
| Ritual complexity | One line + one gesture | 1–2 lines with meaning cue | Phrase + intention + reflective cue |
| Question style | Simple either/or, pointing, naming | Gentle comprehension and application | Reflection-first, perspective-taking, reasoning |
| Parent involvement | Co-read, reassure, model calm | Mirror question + practice cue | Discussion partner; listen, then guide |
| Acceptable ambiguity | Minimal; safe closure needed | Light ambiguity okay with clear value anchor | Moderate ambiguity okay with ethical guardrails |
| What to avoid | Abstract philosophy, burden, fear | Trick questions, moralizing tone | Babyish voice, shame, superiority |

## Safety and Trust Guardrails (All Age Bands)
- No fear-based obedience framing.
- No shame-based morality.
- No spiritual superiority language.
- No “bad child” identity framing.
- No leaderboard/streak/competition framing.
- No public child identity exposure.
- No invented sacred quotes.
- No unsupported scripture claims.
- No casual/decorative sacred-figure usage.

Additional authenticity discipline:
- Use source sensitivity tier labeling consistently.
- Keep source notes conservative and explicit about adaptation.
- Maintain `parentSourceNote` clarity whenever content is interpretive.

## How Templates Support Content Scale
- Planned base story pool: 300–350 `BaseStoryIdea` entries.
- Required expansion: 3 age-band variants each.
- Resulting scale: 900–1,050+ story experiences.

Architecture advantage:
- Private beta can prioritize `ages_6_8` for the core app loop.
- `ages_3_5` and `ages_9_12` can be staged later.
- Because templates are standardized now, delayed age-band rollout does not break long-term index integrity.

---

## Recommended Launch Sequencing
1. **Private beta:** prioritize `ages_6_8` core loop production.
2. **Public beta:** add selected `ages_3_5` parent-read and selected `ages_9_12` deeper variants.
3. **Paid v1:** expand all three age bands across priority worlds.
4. **Long-term moat:** complete age-band coverage across the 1,000+ story experience master index.

## Reviewer Checklist (Per StoryExperience)
- Age fit is clear and developmentally appropriate.
- Primary value is explicit and coherent.
- Tone is emotionally safe (no shame/fear pressure).
- Ritual is appropriate to age and ritual depth target.
- Carrying word/phrase is clear and usable.
- Parent mirror prompt is practical and helpful.
- Bedtime reflection is calming and non-pressuring.
- Source label and source sensitivity tier are correct.
- No unsupported source/scripture claim is present.
- No emotional coercion or moral burden language.
