# Brand and IP Bible

## Brand Personality
Warm, wise, calm, playful, and trustworthy. The tone should feel like a loving family guide with premium design sensibility.

## Visual Principles
- Calm premium palette with ritual warmth.
- Clear hierarchy and restraint over clutter.
- Character-forward storytelling moments.
- Generous spacing and legible typography for parents and kids.
- Motion used for delight and comprehension, not distraction.

## Child-Friendly Tone
- Simple language.
- Encouraging and non-judgmental feedback.
- Wonder-driven prompts.
- Positive reinforcement for effort and curiosity.

## Parent-Trust Tone
- Clear, transparent, and respectful.
- No manipulative urgency.
- Explicit safety and privacy cues.
- Grounded explanations for cultural topics.

## Mascot/IP Direction
- Build a memorable flagship mascot plus companion character set.
- Maintain strict consistency in character voice, visual language, and lore.
- Expand IP through themed worlds, seasonal arcs, and collectible narrative milestones.

## Sound/Audio Identity Direction
- Distinctive sonic logo (gentle and reverent).
- Calm, high-fidelity narration quality.
- Minimal, culturally respectful instrumentation.
- Consistent bedtime/morning ritual sound moods.

## What the App Should Never Feel Like
- No noisy hyper-casual game aesthetic.
- No content farm or random-feed vibe.
- No lecture-heavy classroom stiffness.
- No ad-tech or engagement-trap behavior.
- No culturally shallow or tokenized treatment.
